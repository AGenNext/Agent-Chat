# Autonomyx — Positioning & Messaging

**DRAFT · for founder review.** One-pager that anchors category, audience, and message.

## One-liner
**Autonomyx — the bounded agentic operating system.** It gives you verifiable information from
sources *you* trust and the most efficient tools for the job, with a human always the
accountable Actor.

## Category
Emerging: **Agentic AI platforms / AI-agent operating systems** (Gartner "Agentic AI";
G2 "AI Agents" / "LLM Orchestration"). Autonomyx's wedge inside it is **governance + verified
identity + accountability** — the layer enterprises need before they can adopt agents at all.

## Who it's for
- **Operator (the user):** a professional in a regulated/enterprise context who must stay
  accountable for what the agent does.
- **Buyer:** Head of AI / Platform Eng / CIO / CISO at mid-to-large enterprises that want
  agentic AI **inside their own environment**, governed and auditable.

## The problem
Enterprises want agentic AI but can't safely adopt it: agents are **unbounded**, have **no
verifiable identity or accountability**, create **compliance and trust gaps**, bring **vendor
lock-in**, and **disrupt** existing tools and processes.

## Why Autonomyx (the wedge)
1. **Bounded by verified identity.** The agent acts only within an Operator's verified identity,
   authority, and accountability (Decentralized Identity — DID + wallet + verifiable
   credentials). Accountability pins to a real human.
2. **Honest promise.** It does **not** promise solutions. It promises **verifiable information
   from trusted, user-configured sources** and the **most efficient tools** for a given
   constraint — *intent, not a guarantee*. Defensible, enforceable, no "magic."
3. **Open & self-hostable.** Open-source, CNCF-mature stack, runs in your environment. No vendor
   or licence lock.
4. **Non-disruptive.** Works with your current tools, processes, and people — no re-engineering.
5. **Agent-as-Assistant.** It augments the Operator; it never replaces or acts on its own
   account. Clean separation of **Actor** (human, accountable) and **Tool** (agent).

## We are / We are not
- **We are:** a governed, self-hostable agentic OS that keeps a verified human accountable.
- **We are not:** an autonomous agent that replaces people · a black-box SaaS · a vendor that
  guarantees "solutions."

## Messaging pillars
**Bounded & accountable · Verifiable & trusted · Open & non-disruptive · Human-in-control.**

## Proof points
DCI identity (DID + wallet + VCs) · SurrealDB "fabric" runtime · data layer on Apache JDO ·
Kubernetes-native (k3s) · observability via Apache HertzBeat · conformance/anti-corrosion tested ·
SLSA-grade supply chain (signed, SHA-pinned, SBOM).
